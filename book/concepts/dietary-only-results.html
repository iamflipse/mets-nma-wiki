Context: intermittent fasting node

IF was classified with CR under energy-deficit strategies — consistent with the chrononutrition wiki, IF showed effects on WC/TG/insulin resistance in MetS adults in the included RCTs (e.g. IF: WC MD −2.87 cm, 95% CI −4.79 to −0.95).
