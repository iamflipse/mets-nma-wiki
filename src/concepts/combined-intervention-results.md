## Category-level posterior effects (95% CrI, vs. control)

- **Combined exercise–dietary:** WC −4.41 (−6.78 to −1.99 cm); FBG −6.25 (−11.51 to −1.16 mg/dL); TG −16.58 (−27.55 to −5.71 mg/dL).
- For **WC and TG**, the CNMA showed the combined effect was compatible with the **sum of component effects** (no evidence of synergy) — see [CNMA Additivity](cnma-additivity.md).

**Caveat:** most combined nodes rest on **1–4 studies**; the MICT+LCD FBG signal is a single trial with only 10 participants. These are signals for further direct testing, not established superiority over single-component regimens.
