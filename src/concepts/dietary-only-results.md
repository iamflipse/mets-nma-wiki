# Dietary-Only Results

## Statistically significant control-referenced effects

| Node | Outcome | k (studies) | n (participants) | MD (95% CrI) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CR (caloric restriction) | WC | 9 | 297 | −3.43 cm (−6.14 to −0.70) |
| CR | BMI | 11 | 452 | −1.04 kg/m² (−2.01 to −0.09) |
| LCD (low-carbohydrate) | TG | 3 | 66 | −15.34 mg/dL (−31.13 to −0.53) |
| DASH | WC | 4 | 255 | −4.77 cm (−7.54 to −1.57) |
| DASH | TC (secondary) | 5 | 381 | −18.61 mg/dL (−32.71 to −4.31) |
| DASH | LDL-C (secondary) | 5 | 381 | −13.24 mg/dL (−26.06 to −0.39) |

- **DASH** had the highest dietary-only **SUCRA for WC (0.715)**.
- **No dietary-only node** differed statistically from control for **SBP or DBP**.
- **MedDiet** and **GDC** estimates were not statistically significant for any primary outcome.
- **HPD** and **LFD** were sparse with wide CrIs.

## Context: intermittent fasting node

IF was classified with CR under **energy-deficit strategies** — consistent with the [chrononutrition wiki](https://iamflipse.github.io/if-chrononutrition-wiki/), IF showed effects on WC/TG/insulin resistance in MetS adults in the included RCTs (e.g. IF: WC MD −2.87 cm, 95% CI −4.79 to −0.95).
