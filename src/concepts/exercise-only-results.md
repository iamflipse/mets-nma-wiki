# Exercise-Only Results

## Headline signals (vs. inactive/minimal-intervention control)

- **All 4 structured exercise-only interventions** (MICT, HIIT, RT, COMB-EX) showed **statistically significant reductions in SBP and DBP**.
- **3 of 4** had significant estimates for WC, FBG, and TG; **none** were significant for HDL-C.
- Mean SUCRA among exercise-only nodes was highest for **SBP (0.776)** and **DBP (0.768)** — the blood-pressure signal is the most established effect.

## Most negative control-referenced point estimates

| Node | Outcome | k (studies) | n (participants) | MD (95% CrI) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MICT | SBP | 11 | 375 | −5.42 mmHg (−8.20 to −2.60) |
| COMB-EX | DBP | 9 | 441 | −5.13 mmHg (−8.80 to −1.47) |
| RT | FBG | 7 | 127 | −6.06 mg/dL (−10.74 to −1.35) |
| RT | body weight (secondary) | 5 | 83 | −4.08 kg (−6.39 to −1.28) |

No statistically significant difference was found **among** the active exercise nodes head-to-head.

## Dose–response (per additional 100 MET-min/wk of exposure)

| Outcome | Effect per 100 MET-min/wk | P |
|---|---|---|
| WC | −0.27 cm | <0.001 |
| SBP | −0.43 mmHg | <0.001 |
| DBP | −0.51 mmHg | 0.004 |
| FBG | −0.60 mg/dL | 0.003 |
| TG | −2.32 mg/dL | <0.001 |
| HDL-C | +0.29 mg/dL | 0.014 |

Linear (not spline) fit was preferred; no plateau within the studied exposure ranges.

**Caveats:** most exercise-only estimates rest on 1–11 studies; CINeMA certainty for SBP/DBP/FBG outcomes is low. See [Certainty & Limitations](certainty-and-limitations.md).
