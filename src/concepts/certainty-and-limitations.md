# Certainty, Bias, and Limitations

## CINeMA certainty

- **Low to very low** for most outcome-level comparisons, driven by imprecision, indirectness, and limited direct evidence.
- The MICT-SBP (k=11, n=375), COMB-EX-DBP (k=9, n=441), and RT-FBG (k=7, n=127) nodes had **direct control links**, but outcome-level CINeMA summaries for SBP, DBP, and FBG were **low certainty**.
- Favorable **COMB-EX + MedDiet (WC)** and **MICT + LCD (FBG)** estimates each arose from **1 trial** (190 and 10 participants).

## Publication bias

Funnel-plot asymmetry raised concern for **TG and HDL-C**.

## Sensitivity analyses

- Rankings were **stable** across all 4 sensitivity analyses (no direction reversals).
- Prior-sensitivity showed τ reduction for anthropometric outcomes, especially WC.
- **Leave-one-out:** `study_019` and `study_055` were the most influential trials.
- Excluding sparse nodes changed τ only by small margins.

## Key limitations (Discussion)

1. **Sparse combined nodes:** many combined-intervention nodes relied on few trials and mostly **indirect evidence**; active-vs-active comparisons are less certain than intervention-vs-control.
2. **Additivity restricted:** the CNMA only tested the additive specification; **interaction terms were not estimated**, so the rejected additivity for SBP/DBP/FBG/HDL-C indicates model misfit but doesn't identify which component pairs are synergistic or antagonistic.
3. **Duration:** median study duration 16 wk → estimates mainly reflect short-to-medium-term effects; HDL-C changes may be under-detected.
4. **Eligibility:** adults with MetS **and BMI ≥25 kg/m²** → limits generalizability to MetS adults with lower BMI.
5. **Harmonization gaps:** differences in supervision, adherence, diet composition, energy deficit, and background medication could not be fully harmonized.
6. **Sex-specificity:** only 2 trials reported sex-stratified baselines → no meaningful sex-specific synthesis or sex-by-intervention interaction.
