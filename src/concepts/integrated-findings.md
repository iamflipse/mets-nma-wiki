# Integrated Findings & Research Agenda

## What the network shows (Figure 5 integrated summary)

- **No single intervention ranked first for all 6 outcomes.**
- **Most established signal:** exercise → blood pressure. All 4 structured exercise-only nodes improved SBP and DBP.
- **Selected combined regimens** produced further signals: COMB-EX + MedDiet (WC), MICT + CR (TG), MICT + LCD (FBG).
- **Additivity held only for WC and TG**; for SBP, DBP, FBG, HDL-C the additive model did not fit.
- **No preferred regimen** and **no general advantage of combined over single-component** interventions is established.

## Research agenda (Conclusion)

The evidence — mostly **low/very low certainty**, with few direct active comparisons — identifies **which comparisons warrant adequately powered, longer-follow-up head-to-head trials**:

1. Direct testing of the favorable **combined regimens** (COMB-EX+MedDiet, MICT+CR, MICT+LCD) vs. single-component regimens.
2. Longer trials to resolve **HDL-C** and sustained lipid effects.
3. Consistent reporting of **intervention delivery and adherence** (supervision, fidelity).
4. **Sex-stratified** outcomes (only 2/193 arms reported sex-specific baselines).

## Divergence from earlier syntheses

Earlier comparative syntheses found multimodal interventions outperforming nutrition-only for MetS reversal; this review's more granular nodes (individual diagnostic components + specific regimens) produced **outcome-specific** patterns instead of a single "best" intervention. This aligns with a recent direct meta-analysis showing adding exercise to diet improved WC and FBG but not HDL-C, BP, or TG.
